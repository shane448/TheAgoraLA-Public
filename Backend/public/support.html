Listener Support

Agora turns the podcasts you choose into focused learning sessions grounded in the episode itself.

Need help? Email support@theagorala.com with the podcast URL, device model, iOS version, and a short description of the issue.

Import an episode

Copy a public episode or show link from Apple Podcasts or another supported podcast page, paste it into Add Episode, then choose Import, Analyze & Save. Show links open a quick episode picker when a feed can be discovered.

Transcript and questions

Agora imports an available transcript or transcribes accessible episode audio, reads the full transcript, writes a listener-facing summary, and selects the strongest episode-specific questions. Open an episode and choose View Full Transcript whenever a transcript is available.

AI access

The Agora is free and does not sell credits. Open AI Setup and choose Connect Automatically, approve The Agora, choose analysis quality, and confirm that setup is ready. If you already created a regular OpenRouter API key, choose I Already Have an API Key, paste it, and choose Verify & Connect. If the provider reports insufficient credits, use the app's Open Provider Credits button, manage that account directly with the provider, and retry.

Disconnect AI

Open AI Setup and choose Disconnect AI Account. This removes the provider credential from the iPhone Keychain. The Agora does not maintain a separate listener account.

Before contacting support

  • Confirm the podcast page and episode audio are publicly accessible.
  • Keep Agora open while a new episode is being analyzed.
  • Try the same episode again after a temporary provider or network error.
  • Include the podcast URL, device model, iOS version, and exact error message when reporting a repeatable issue.
