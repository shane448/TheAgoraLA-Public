# In-App Purchases

The Agora LA has no in-app purchases, subscriptions, paid unlocks, or Agora-issued credits.

Listeners may authorize a separate AI-provider account through OpenRouter OAuth. Any provider usage or billing relationship belongs directly to the listener and the provider. The Agora does not sell, broker, or receive payment for that service and does not include a purchase link or call to action inside the app.

Do not create In-App Purchase products for version 1.0 and do not sign Apple's Paid Applications Agreement for this app.
