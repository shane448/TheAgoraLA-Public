# App Review Notes

The Agora LA is a free podcast-learning app. A listener pastes a public podcast or episode URL, authorizes an AI-provider account they control, and asks the app to prepare a brief, full transcript, and episode-specific listening questions. The listener answers in their own words and receives transcript-grounded feedback.

The Agora does not create listener accounts, sell credits, offer subscriptions, unlock paid features, or receive payment from the AI provider. The app contains no purchase flow or purchase call to action.

## Review Without an External Account

1. Launch the app.
2. Choose **Explore a Sample Lesson**.
3. Read the included brief and open **View Full Transcript**.
4. Choose **Answer Sample Question**.
5. Enter an answer and choose **Submit** to see offline scoring, feedback, the podcast-supported answer, and Agora Points.

The sample lesson and its transcript were written by The Agora LA and are included specifically so App Review can inspect the core learning experience without an external account, payment, or copyrighted podcast audio.

## Optional Live-AI Review

1. Open **AI Setup** and choose **Connect Automatically**.
2. Sign in or create an OpenRouter account and approve the connection.
3. Select analysis quality and choose **Return to The Agora**.
4. Paste a public podcast URL in **Edit Episode & Prompts**.
5. Choose **Import, Analyze & Save**.
6. Review the generated brief, transcript, questions, expected answers, and answer feedback.

OpenRouter creates a user-controlled provider credential. It remains in the iPhone Keychain. AI requests go directly from the device to the authorized provider; The Agora does not operate a shared AI account for listeners. A reviewer who already has a regular OpenRouter API key may instead choose **I Already Have an API Key**, paste it, and choose **Verify & Connect**.

## Third-Party Content

The app does not provide a browsable podcast catalog and does not publicly redistribute transcripts. Processing is initiated by the listener for a public link they supply, and generated study material remains private on that device.
